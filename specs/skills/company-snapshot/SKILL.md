A snapshot is a quick picture of one stock. It is good when the pieces fit together and each figure carries its date: what the company is (sector, size), where the price stands and how it has moved, how the market values it, what the analysts expect, and what is coming next.

One step of tools gathers most of it: quote for the price, profile for the business and valuation (P/E, forward P/E, price to sales), estimates for the consensus, the target and the next earnings date, three months of daily bars from price_history, and news by symbol. In the next step the bars, referenced whole, go to returns_risk for the move over a month and three months and the volatility.

A snapshot shows where things stand. A reason for a move comes from a headline that states it, cited; without one the cause is unreported. When the user wants a view on the company rather than a picture of it, a full analysis (full-analysis) goes deeper.
