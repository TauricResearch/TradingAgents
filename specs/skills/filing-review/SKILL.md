A filing review reads what a company discloses and what changed. It is good when each point rests on the text, quoted or closely paraphrased and cited, so the reader can find it in the filing.

filings lists the company's 10-Ks and 10-Qs; the latest one the question needs and the same form a year earlier make the comparison. filing_section reads a section part by part, and each part's continue line leads to the rest. risk_factors and mdna in both filings show risks added, removed or reworded with new weight, and changes in what management says drives results, guidance and liquidity.

Each point carries the form, accession, period and filing date of its filing. A filing supports what it says, so the review names the sections and parts it read; a part left unread is a gap in the comparison, not evidence that nothing changed there.
