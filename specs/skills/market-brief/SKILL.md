A market brief says what moved in the latest session and why. It is good when the largest moves lead and each one carries its reason, so the reader sees the session's story rather than a list of prices.

markets gives the session in one step: equity_summary, indices, sectors, gainers, losers and cross_asset, with news without a symbol for the market's latest (impact high). For the two or three largest moves, news by symbol and article for the story that explains most give the reasons; the rates, commodities or crypto views add the rest when they moved.

The session date belongs with the figures. During a session, the quote time and the move so far describe where things stand, since nothing has closed. A reason for a move comes from a news result; without one, the cause is unreported. One session is a snapshot: how a sector has done over weeks or months is a sector review's question (sector-review).
