## Decisions

- **Inline ANSI dots over Gum `--foreground`**: Gum's `--foreground` applies to
  the entire block. Per-row colour requires inline ANSI escapes. This is
  documented in the gum playbook.
- **Dynamic width over `--width`**: `--width` is fine for fixed content but
  fails when detail strings vary (PIDs, paths). `padEnd()` math adapts.
- **Title and hint outside the box**: Prevents wrapping of decorative text.

## What Was Avoided

- Direct editing of `server-lifecycle.ts` before proving the pattern in lab
- Nested borders, fixed-width boxes with variable content
- Adding more services to a broken display (would compound the problem)
