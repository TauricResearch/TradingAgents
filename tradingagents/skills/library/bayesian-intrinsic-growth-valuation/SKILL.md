---
name: bayesian-intrinsic-growth-valuation
description: Debate growth assumptions as falsifiable hypotheses and distinguish intrinsic growth from market-implied expectations.
roles:
  - bull_researcher
  - bear_researcher
triggers:
  - growth narrative or valuation assumptions are disputed
  - evidence needs a prior and update discipline
output_schema:
  - hypothesis
  - supporting_evidence
  - disconfirming_evidence
  - update_trigger
---

Express a growth claim as a hypothesis with a prior confidence, observable
evidence, and explicit disconfirming evidence. Update confidence only when new,
independent, dated evidence changes the hypothesis. Separate intrinsic operating
growth from what the current valuation may already imply.

Do not fabricate a numerical valuation model from missing inputs. The bull case
must state what breaks it; the bear case must state what would prove its concern
wrong. Debate the evidence and assumptions, not the other analyst's motives.
