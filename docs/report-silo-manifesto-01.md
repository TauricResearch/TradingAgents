# The Silo Manifesto

*A Protocol for Competent Agentic Cooperation*

## I. The Core Tenet: The Silo as Constant

The Silo is the primary artifact of all work. It is not a temporary workspace; it is a permanent, audit-ready container that persists through time, surviving the turnover of models, agent sessions, and human cycles. We do not "babysit" an agent; we curate an environment.

## II. The Architecture of Competence

We reject the "Babysitter" pattern. The Babysitter paradigm assumes agent incompetence and attempts to rectify it through restrictive runtime interference. Instead, we operate on the assumption of **Agentic Competence**:

* **Environment-Driven Alignment**: By embedding our conventions, lexicon, and history directly into the Silo (via `briefs`, `decisions`, `playbooks`, and `lexicons`), agents become aligned simply by observing the Silo's state.


* **Deterministic Foundation**: We favor deterministic processes (`just` recipes, CLI scripts) over agentic improvisation wherever possible.


* **Visitor Accountability**: Agents are visiting experts, not owners. Every agent "signs in" to the `visitors-book.md`, committing to uphold the established Silo protocols.



## III. The Human-Agent Feedback Loop

We acknowledge the cognitive duality of our work:

* **Human System-1**: The creative leaps, nuances, and insights are the human's domain. We do not try to automate the "spark"; we provide a place to house it.


* **Agent System-2**: The agent is our Steward. It handles the housekeeping, the ADR drafting, the registry synchronization, and the cross-referencing of new ideas against the canonical lexicon.


* **Cooperation over Supervision**: The Human provides the *Intent*, the Agent provides the *Execution*, and the Silo provides the *Truth*.



## IV. The Integrity Standard (Edinburgh Protocol)

Operational accountability is not optional. Every significant task is subject to the **Edinburgh Protocol**:

* **Transparency**: No "black box" decisions; every architectural shift is captured in an ADR.


* **Traceability**: Every execution is traceable back to a Brief and forward to a Debrief.


* **Hygiene**: We actively scrape "process barnacles"—stale documentation, orphaned branches, and misleading indices—to ensure the agent always starts from a state of total clarity.



## V. Our Commitment

We build for longevity and integrity. We recognize that the most expensive part of agentic AI is not the token cost, but the *alignment cost* of poorly structured projects. By investing in the Silo, we ensure that our project grows smarter, more consistent, and more capable with every session, regardless of which model is occupying the seat.
