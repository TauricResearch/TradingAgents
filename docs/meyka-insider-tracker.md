I analyzed 1,000 insider trades and the results were surprising. Here is what the data actually says about how insiders buy and sell stock.

Most traders watch charts. Some read earnings reports. But very few check what company insiders are actually doing with their own money.

We decided to dig into the data. We pulled the latest 1,000 insider trades from our database and ran the numbers. What we found will change how you think about insider trading data.

## What Is Insider Trading Data?

Before we get into the findings, let us be clear. We are not talking about illegal insider trading. We are talking about legal transactions that company executives, directors, and major shareholders must report to the SEC.

Every time a CEO, CFO, or board member buys or sells shares in their own company, they must file a Form 4 with the SEC within two business days. This data is public. Anyone can access it.

Most retail investors never look at it. That is a mistake.

**Source:** [SEC EDGAR Form 4 Filings](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&type=4)
