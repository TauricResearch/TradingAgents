Test-driven clarifications documented
-------------------------------------
- The 20% concentration cap applies to position-increasing BUY orders. SELL is
  risk-reducing and exempt from the concentration cap, while the 10% notional cap
  still applies to both BUY and SELL (pinned by ACT-15/FND-23 and FND-22).
- A stale caller snapshot whose proposal version matches the DB is
  REJECTED/STALE_PORTFOLIO. A proposal that loses a concurrent portfolio-version
  race returns the distinct status STALE_PORTFOLIO; no ledger write (pinned by
  ATM-01 vs ATM-07/ATM-09).
- HOLD requires a valid evidence pack; claims are optional because no order is
  created. BUY/SELL require at least one claim with resolvable evidence.
- Exactly one outbox event per stored verdict (Phase.RISK_GATE), matching the
  duplicate/idempotency tests.
