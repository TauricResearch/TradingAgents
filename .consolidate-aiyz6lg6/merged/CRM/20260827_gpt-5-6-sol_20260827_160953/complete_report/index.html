  • Deep integration into mission-critical business operations
  • Cross-selling opportunities through Slack, data, analytics, and AI

AI may replace individual software features, but enterprises still need trusted systems for customer data, permissions, compliance, workflow orchestration, and measurable business outcomes. CRM already owns a significant portion of that enterprise layer.
