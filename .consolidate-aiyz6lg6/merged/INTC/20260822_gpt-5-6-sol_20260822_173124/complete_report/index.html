INTC reported a Q2 net loss of $11.03 billion, which looks alarming in isolation. However, the quarter included approximately $12.74 billion of unusual items, including major write-offs and investment-related effects.

The bear may say INTC is losing ground to stronger competitors. That risk is real. Dilution destroys value only if the capital is deployed into projects that fail to earn an adequate return.
