Bull Researcher¶

Bull Analyst: # Bull Case for SPCX

Let's start with what actually matters for a company at this stage of its lifecycle: is the growth real, and is the balance sheet strong enough to fund it to profitability? On both counts, the answer is a resounding yes.

The Growth Story Is Accelerating, Not Slowing¶

Q2 2026 revenue hit $7.81B, up 66% quarter-over-quarter from $4.69B in Q1. That's not incremental growth — that's exponential scaling in a company operating in a market (commercial launch, satellite broadband, space infrastructure) with almost no ceiling in sight. Gross margins sit at a healthy ~52%, proving the underlying unit economics work once you strip out the capex and R&D drag.

And crucially — losses are collapsing. Net loss narrowed from -$4.28B in Q1 to just -$541M in Q2. That's an ~87% improvement in one quarter. Operating cash flow flipped positive to +$2.42B. This is a company crossing the inflection point from "cash-burning moonshot" to "operationally self-sustaining growth machine" in real time.

The Balance Sheet Just Got Bulletproof¶

The bears will point to negative free cash flow (-$16.8B) and rising debt. But look at what actually happened this quarter: SPCX raised $85.7B in equity, pushing cash to $100B and stockholders' equity to $127.2B — nearly tripling QoQ. Current ratio of 5.1x is exceptional. This isn't a company scrambling for survival capital — it's a company with a war chest large enough to fund years of Starship-scale infrastructure buildout without needing to touch capital markets under duress. The capex intensity ($18-19B/quarter) is a choice to dominate the space economy, not a symptom of distress.
