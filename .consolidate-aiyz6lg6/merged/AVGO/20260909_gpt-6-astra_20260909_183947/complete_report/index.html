1. AI opportunity remains large, but forecasts are long-dated¶

  • Greater pricing pressure.
  • More volatile revenue timing.
  • Higher customer-concentration risk.

This is not necessarily a thesis break. Hyperscaler internal design can still require external partners for manufacturing coordination, packaging, networking, connectivity, and production scaling. However, the market may react negatively to any evidence that internal chip programs reduce AVGO’s addressable opportunity.

A recent report says Truist reduced its AVGO target following concerns about the Q4 outlook. Another headline says AVGO shares slipped while its revenue schedule became longer.
