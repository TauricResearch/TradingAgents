Bull Analyst: ## Opening bull case: AMZN

I’m bullish on AMZN as a multiyear investment, though I would not mistake the current pullback for an automatic buy signal. The case is that its operating business is improving at enormous scale, while the market’s most legitimate concern—investment spending—is visible and testable.
