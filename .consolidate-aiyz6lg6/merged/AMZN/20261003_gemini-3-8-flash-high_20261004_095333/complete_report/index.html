Indicator Selection Rationale¶

To deliver comprehensive, non-redundant coverage across trend, momentum, volatility, volume, and exhaustion, eight complementary indicators were selected:

  3. Relative Strength Index (rsi) [Momentum / Oscillators]: Measures the magnitude of recent gains against losses on a pure price basis (avoiding volume blending to complement OBV/MFI) to detect overbought/oversold boundaries.
  4. MACD Histogram (macdh) [Momentum Acceleration / Divergence]: Tracks changes in the rate of momentum. Selected over the raw MACD line to capture early momentum inflections and potential bullish crossovers ahead of price breakouts.
  5. Average True Range (atr) [Volatility / Risk Management]: Quantifies absolute daily trading ranges in dollar terms ($5.63), facilitating objective risk-sizing and stop-loss placement independent of market direction.
  6. On-Balance Volume (obv) [Volume Accumulation / Distribution]: Tracks cumulative signed volume to evaluate whether institutional capital is accumulating shares during consolidations or quietly distributing into bounces.
  7. TD Sequential Setup (td_9) [Sequential / Exhaustion]: Provides multi-timeframe DeMark countdown sequences (Weekly, Monthly, Daily) to flag approaching cycle exhaustion points and reversal inflection windows.
  8. Z-Score (z_score) [Mean Reversion / Statistical Stretch]: Measures how many standard deviations the close sits from its 20-period mean across weekly, monthly, and daily tiers, identifying whether the stock is stretched or at fair value.
