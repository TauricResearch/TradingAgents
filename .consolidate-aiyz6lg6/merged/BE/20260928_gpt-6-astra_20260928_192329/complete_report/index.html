Bull Researcher¶

Bull Analyst: My opening case for BE: the stock is expensive and volatile, but the business is now showing the revenue growth, operating leverage and cash generation that a serious bull thesis requires. I would not mistake Monday’s selloff for evidence that those operating gains disappeared.

Start with the numbers. Q2 2026 revenue reached $1.065 billion, up 165.5% year over year. Gross margin rose from 26.7% to 33.4%, operating income improved from a $3.5 million loss to a $182.2 million profit, and free cash flow swung from negative $220.4 million to positive $174.8 million. That is more than a promising demand story: BE has demonstrated that substantially higher sales can translate into better margins and cash flow. As an illustration, if each of the next two quarters merely matched Q2 revenue, full-year 2026 revenue would be about $3.95 billion, versus $2.02 billion in 2025. That is a scenario, not guidance.

The opportunity is credible, too. AI data centers need large amounts of dependable power, and BE’s on-site fuel-cell systems offer customers a way to address that need where site infrastructure permits. Secondary reports say Oracle remains committed to an approximately 2.4 GW arrangement. I would not call all 2.4 GW guaranteed near-term revenue, but it demonstrates the scale of demand BE is being considered to serve. BE’s strongest competitive argument is its ability to deliver on-site generation at scale; the financial results suggest it is gaining operating leverage as it grows.
